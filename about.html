Who We Are

Alafia Point Wellness Clinic

Alafia Point Wellness Clinic is a leading physiotherapy and nutrition practice based on Lukuli Rd, Kampala, Uganda. We are dedicated to delivering evidence-based, patient-centred care that restores health, prevents injury, and empowers every individual to live their best life.

Our multidisciplinary team of physiotherapists, nutritionists, and wellness coaches work together to provide holistic treatment plans tailored to each patient's unique needs — whether recovering from injury, managing a chronic condition, or simply pursuing a healthier lifestyle.

Founded with the belief that "a healthy community begins with one individual at a time," we strive to make quality healthcare accessible to every person in Kampala and beyond.
